Aiko Tanaka founded Nova Labs in Kyoto in 2019. Nova Labs specializes in autonomous drone navigation software for agriculture. Dr. Elena Reyes joined Nova Labs as Chief Scientist in 2021. Nova Labs partners with Orion Dynamics on a joint research initiative called Project Kestrel. Project Kestrel aims to build a swarm coordination system for agricultural drones. Marcus Webb leads the engineering team behind Project Kestrel. Orion Dynamics is based in Singapore and manufactures industrial sensors. Helio Ventures invested five million dollars in Nova Labs during a funding round in 2022. Briar Robotics later acquired a minority stake in Orion Dynamics. Aiko Tanaka previously worked at Briar Robotics before starting Nova Labs. Dr. Elena Reyes collaborated with Marcus Webb on the swarm coordination algorithms used in Project Kestrel. Nova Labs opened a second office in Osaka in 2023. The Osaka office focuses on testing Project Kestrel prototypes in real farm environments. Helio Ventures also backs Briar Robotics through an earlier investment fund. Marcus Webb previously worked at Orion Dynamics before joining Nova Labs in 2020.
